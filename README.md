## About

I am a technology entrepreneur, AI systems builder and independent researcher. My work covers decision infrastructure, autonomous systems and verifiable computing, with a focus on how organisations keep control over actions taken by AI, software and people.

At Parmana Systems I am developing deterministic authorisation, execution integrity and cryptographically verifiable evidence. The aim is to turn business intent into enforceable rules, so that authorisation is explicit, execution is predictable and outcomes can be verified independently.

## Current focus

> What can an AI agent do, and how do we prove it?
>
> A prompt instruction is not a control. What holds up is a check before each action that a person has approved, and a record a third party can verify without trusting the system that produced it.

## Research

- **[The Authority Gap: Why Autonomous AI Needs Deterministic Execution Boundaries](https://github.com/pavancharak/parmana-core/blob/claude/great-darwin-j9noof/research/the-authority-gap.pdf)** (discussion draft, November 2026). It argues that an institution's authority over an AI agent must be declared independently, decided deterministically and enforced at the point of execution, with the evidence kept verifiable. The paper is available as a PDF in the `research/` folder of the [parmana-core](https://github.com/pavancharak/parmana-core) repository.
- **[Beyond Trust But Verify: Why Autonomous AI Needs Verifiable Execution](https://github.com/pavancharak/parmana-core/pull/606)** (research paper, pull request, November 2026). It argues that authority over autonomous AI must be explicit, independently evaluated and verifiable, and cites the Carnegie report and California's frontier AI laws.
